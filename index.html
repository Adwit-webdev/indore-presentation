Key Findings

✓ Strengths

  • 85.16% effective literacy
  • 100% door-to-door collection
  • Zero-landfill city
  • 16.7× biomethanation jump
  • 8 consecutive Swachh Rank 1

⚠ Concerns

  • Child sex ratio: 897
  • WPR gap: 56.32% vs 14.65%
  • 29.60% slum population
  • ST share: 2.61% vs 21.09%
  • Waste data not updated since 2019

Conclusion

Indore is a rare case where rapid demographic growth and rapid service improvement moved together — but deep spatial inequities remain.
